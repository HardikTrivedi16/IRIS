## IMPORTANT — correction to the research pack

Circular 257 does **not** contain a "₹20 crore" figure anywhere (checked by
full-text search of the extracted text). The export-credit collateral
support it introduces operates **within the existing ₹10 crore overall
guarantee ceiling** (explicit: "...within the overall ceiling of ₹10
crore"), expressed as an **additional coverage percentage** — 85% total for
Micro & Small exporters (75% base + 10% additional), 65% for Medium
exporters — not a raised rupee ceiling. The research pack's "exporter cover
to ₹20 cr reported 2026 — verify at archival" is **not supported** by this
document and should not be carried into the scheme record as stated.
